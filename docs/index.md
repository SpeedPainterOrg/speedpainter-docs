# SpeedPainter Documentation

**[SpeedPainter](https://www.speedpainter.org/en)** turns scripts, documentation, diagrams, and text notes into narrated hand-drawn whiteboard explainer videos and AI drawing timelapses.

## Core Platform Guides

- **[SpeedPainter Home](https://www.speedpainter.org/en)** — Create narrated whiteboard animations and speed-painting timelapses online.
- **[Whiteboard Video Maker](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/whiteboard-video-maker)** — Build step-by-step illustrated whiteboard videos directly from text scripts.
- **[AI Drawing Timelapse Generator](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/ai-drawing-timelapse-generator)** — Turn concepts and sketches into fluid hand-drawn speed-painting timelapses.
- **[Hand-Drawn Explainer Videos](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video)** — End-to-end narrated explainer workflow with synchronized captions.
- **[Product Explainer Videos](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video/product)** — Communicate SaaS features, architecture, and onboarding flows clearly.
- **[Education Explainer Videos](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video/education)** — Visual lessons and classroom concept breakdowns.
- **[Training Explainer Videos](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video/training)** — Repeatable team SOPs and internal training modules.
- **[Agent Skill & MCP Integration](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/skills/explainer-video)** — Connect Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor directly to SpeedPainter.
- **[Pricing & Plans](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/pricing)** — Credit tiers and commercial licensing.

## Quick Start

1. Prepare a concise script or document outline (typically 5–8 visual beats for a 30–45 second clip).
2. Choose an output aspect ratio (`16:9` for YouTube/docs, `9:16` for Shorts/Reels, or `1:1` for social feeds).
3. Generate scenes, narration, and burned-in subtitles via the [SpeedPainter web studio](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/generate) or the [Explainer Video MCP server](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/skills/explainer-video).
