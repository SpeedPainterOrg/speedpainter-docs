# Whiteboard & Explainer Video Workflows

This guide outlines practical production patterns for creating clear, engaging visual explainers with [SpeedPainter](https://www.speedpainter.org/en).

## Script Structure for Hand-Drawn Pacing

Hand-drawn whiteboard pacing works best when each spoken sentence maps to one distinct visual metaphor (~5–7 seconds per scene):

| Video Length | Recommended Scenes | Word Count (EN) | Best Use Case |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 30 seconds | 5 scenes | 70–85 words | [Product launch teasers](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video/product) |
| 60 seconds | 9–10 scenes | 140–160 words | [Feature walkthroughs](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/whiteboard-video-maker) |
| 90 seconds | 14–15 scenes | 210–240 words | [Education & training lessons](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video/education) |

## Recommended Workflows

- **Documentation to Video**: Paste an onboarding doc or architecture overview into the [Whiteboard Video Maker](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/whiteboard-video-maker) to generate storyboarded hand-drawn scenes with narration.
- **Diagram to Speed-Painting Timelapse**: Use the [AI Drawing Timelapse Generator](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/ai-drawing-timelapse-generator) to animate progressive stroke drawing for tutorials and social clips.
- **Internal Training Rollouts**: Standardize SOP explanations across teams using [SpeedPainter Training Videos](https://www.speedpainter.org/en/explainer-video/training).
